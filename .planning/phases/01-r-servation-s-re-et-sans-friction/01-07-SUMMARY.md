# Phase 1 Plan 07: OTP-free reservation form Summary

**Single-step reservation form (locale-preselected international phone, required email, unticked consent, honeypot, translated errors, WhatsApp fallback on 429) and complete removal of the Twilio SMS backend.**

## Tasks

1. **Form rewrite** - commit `c9758db`. TDD: `tests/reservation-form.test.ts` failed (9 tests) before, passes after.
2. **SMS backend removal** - commit `3471b0b`. `tests/deps.test.ts` extended first (2 failing), green after.

## What changed

- `ReservationForm.tsx`: all OTP state/UI removed. Country initial value from `phoneCountryForLocale(useLocale())` (FR on /fr, GB on /en). Email `required`, hint after blur. Date `min` = `bangkokNow().date`; time slots from `bookableSlots()` fed by a one-time `GET /api/settings` (default grid until loaded). Party stepper 1..40 with 44px buttons and i18n aria-labels. Consent checkbox `res-consent`, initial `false`, reset after success, privacy link via locale-aware `Link` with `rel="noopener"`. Honeypot `company_url` off-screen and `aria-hidden`; `form_started_at` stored in a ref set on mount. Submit button `id="reservation-submit"`; wrapper `pb-24 sm:pb-10`. Errors in a `role="alert"` banner; 429 adds a prefilled WhatsApp link. Success panel has `role="status"` and focuses its heading.
- `messages/fr.json`, `en.json`: `otp*` keys removed, UI-SPEC copy added (including `datePast`); `email` label now "Email *" and `required` note no longer says confirmation is by phone.
- Removed: `/api/verify/*`, `isOtpCode` (and its test block), `PhoneVerification` type, `twilio` (package.json diff is the single twilio line), CLAUDE.md twilio and verify references.

## Deviations from Plan

- Success note and large-group warning no longer carry emoji (UI-SPEC copy).
