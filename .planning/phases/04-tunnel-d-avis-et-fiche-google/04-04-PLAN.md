## Phase Goal

Chaque client honoré est invité à laisser un avis Google ou TripAdvisor, et la fiche Google est optimisée pour la haute saison.

(Slice delivered by plans 04-04 + 04-05: **an honored guest receives the J+1 invitation and at most one J+5 reminder**. This TDD plan builds the decision logic and the message; 04-05 wires the cron that sends them.)

<objective>
Write migration 014 and the pure, unit-tested core of the invitation flow: Krabi date math, eligibility selection for invite and reminder, email locale fallback, email link builder, and the FR/EN invitation/reminder email template.

Purpose: REV-02 (honored by default = computed eligibility, no new status) and REV-03 (timing, locale, single reminder). The risky parts (UTC+7 windows, eligibility) are isolated in pure functions so the cron route stays thin.
Output: 014 migration, types, review-invitations.ts, reviewInvitationEmail, three test files.

Decision recorded (RESEARCH finding, refines D-14): `gecko_reservations.id` is an integer (enumerable), so `rid` is the new opaque `review_token uuid` column, not the id.
First-run policy (Open Question 3, decided here): NO backfill of review_*_sent_at. Writing fake send dates would show false "Invitation envoyée" badges. Exposure is bounded by D-07 (only service dates 1-3 days before the first run can be invited; reminders require a recorded invite, so no pre-existing row can ever get a reminder). Plan 04-07 runs the cron in dry-run mode first and shows the owner the exact count before the first real send.
</objective>
