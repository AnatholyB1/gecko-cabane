## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor or guest of Gecko Cabane, **I want to** subscribe from the website, the bill QR or the WiFi page and confirm my email once, **so that** I receive the Krabi food guide and relevant news from the restaurant.

Slice delivered by this plan: **after a guest dines, their Brevo profile shows the visit date, country and resident flag so the J+2/J+3 sequences start on their own; past reservation-form consents get one chance to confirm.**

| server -> Brevo | personal data (visit date, country, resident) to processor |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-09-01 | Spoofing | cron endpoints | mitigate | Bearer CRON_SECRET checked before any work, fail closed when unset |
| T-07-09-02 | Tampering | re-adding unsubscribed contacts | mitigate | sync PUTs attributes only, never listIds; only actives (confirmed after last withdrawal) are processed |
| T-07-09-03 | Information disclosure | cron responses | mitigate | counts only, never emails |
| T-07-09-04 | Repudiation | D-03 mailing | mitigate | claim confirmation_sent_at per row, once per email ever, manual call after owner approval, dry-run count first |
| T-07-09-05 | Denial of service | Brevo/Resend rate limits | mitigate | 150 ms / 600 ms pauses, 200-call and 50-mail caps per run, maxDuration 60 |
| T-07-09-06 | Information disclosure | data minimisation | mitigate | only email, visit date, country and resident flag leave for Brevo; no name, no phone |
